# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS (one `index.html` + one stylesheet, no build step), chosen so GitHub Pages serves it directly and the owner can edit it like a text file. Proposed in chat and approved by the owner.

## Users

Academic researchers, mainly in human-robot interaction: faculty, potential collaborators, reviewers, and conference contacts who heard a name and want to know who this person is. They arrive from a paper, a CV, LinkedIn or a conference badge and give the page about a minute.

## Product Purpose

Personal academic website for Phurinat (Chester) Pinyomit. After one visit a researcher should know who he is, what he works on right now, and where his research is headed. Success: they can place him (lab, field, stage), find his publication, and reach him or his CV in one click.

## Positioning

An HRI researcher whose work makes robots easier to live alongside by using playful, embodied channels (puppetry, storytelling) and by pinning down what makes a robot feel social. Vision, in the owner's words: we can co-exist with robots more easily, comfortably, and trust them.

## Operating Context

- Hosted on GitHub Pages at `https://chester853.github.io` from repo `CHester853/CHester853.github.io`.
- Read on laptops at a desk and on phones at conferences.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One scrolling page: hero, about, current research, publications, past projects, news, footer.
- Current research is described **generically only**. The storytelling-robot system details (e.g. Chatterbox TTS, pipeline) and the sociability scoping review (research questions, co-authors, methods) are unpublished and must not appear.
- Open: Google Scholar URL, headshot photo.

## Brand Commitments

- Tone: stands out, professional, and warm / welcoming. First-person "Hi, I'm Chester."

## Product Principles

1. Who, what now, where next: in that order, within the first screen and a scroll.
2. Never reveal unpublished work beyond a one-line general description.
3. Every claim traces to the CV, the paper or the owner's words.
4. Easy for the owner to update by hand (news line, new paper).
